# March for Billionaires

The **March for Billionaires** took place on Saturday, February 7, 2026, in San Francisco. It was a small, earnest rally and march protesting a proposed California "billionaire wealth tax" (a ballot measure potentially taxing a percentage of ultra-high net worth individuals' total assets, which critics argue could drive capital flight and hurt the state's economy).

**Purpose**  
The group's website and [Twitter bio](https://x.com/probillionaires) framed the event as a "celebration of billionaires' contributions" to prosperity, innovation, and California's status as a major economic powerhouse. Key slogans included "Billionaires Build Prosperity" and opposition to vilifying or taxing the ultra-wealthy, emphasizing that losing billionaires (via relocation) costs the state significant tax revenue.

**Scale and activities**

- The march began around 11 a.m. at Alta Plaza Park in Pacific Heights (a wealthy neighborhood fittingly symbolic).
- It proceeded through the area and ended with a rally at San Francisco City Hall / Civic Center.
- Signs and chants defended billionaires' role in creating jobs, innovation, and wealth (e.g., one sign reportedly read something like "We love you Jeffrey Bezos").
- @ProBillionaires posted photos from the day showing a small group marching, holding signs, and thanking attendees, with the bio highlighting "Feb 7 2026" as the event date.

**Reception and media coverage**

- Significant local and national media (SF Chronicle, LA Times, SF Examiner, SFGATE, Mission Local, SFist, NY Post, and others).
- Counter-protesters, including a "March for Trillionaires"
- Some critics on Twitter questioned why non-billionaires would advocate for the ultra-rich

Post-event, @ProBillionaires shared thank-you messages, photos, and continued defending the cause (e.g., arguing wealth isn't zero-sum and citing examples like Sweden repealing a wealth tax due to capital flight).
