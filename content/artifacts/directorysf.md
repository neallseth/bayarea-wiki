# DirectorySF

DirectorySF is a referral-only social directory and housing platform catering to the Bay Area's [megascene](/megascene).

## Goals

The project's [aim](https://x.com/neallseth/status/1793757914542317892) is to increase serendipity and experimentation in San Francisco, by increasing social density:

> incredible things emerge from dense clusters of optimistic, curious, ambitious people — things that simply wouldn’t emerge in isolation

What _specifically_ emerges - art projects, technologies, institutions, or novel ideologies - is less of a concern. The more important thing is that our species be _generative_ in interesting and positive ways.

## Active domains

DirectorySF is accessible at [directorysf.com](https://directorysf.com) and [directorysf.org](https://directorysf.org)
